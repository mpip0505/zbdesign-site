2# ZB Design: Improved Website Copy (2 pages)

Source: the current site at zbdesign.com.my (Home, About Us, Services, Workflow, Gallery, Contact, plus a sample of project pages). This deck rewrites that content for the new two-page design: **Home** and **Projects**. The Workflow page is dropped, as the designer suggested.

Anything in `[brackets]` is a fact I could not confirm from the current site. Ask the client before launch.

## 1. What I found on the current site

**Who they are.** ZB Design Sdn. Bhd. positions itself as an **interior design and renovation company** that specialises in **smart home systems**, based in Taman Melawati, Kuala Lumpur. It serves homes and commercial spaces. It is not described as an architecture practice, so the placeholder wording in the design (architecture, masterplanning, heritage, cultural buildings) should be replaced with the copy below.

**Things to confirm with the client**
1. **Years of experience.** The Home page says 25 years; the Services page says over 30. Use one number.
2. **Credentials.** All pages mention an architectural certification, smart home system competency and a licensed construction specialist licence. No names or registration numbers are given. Ask for the exact titles (and any registration number) so they can be shown properly.
3. **Home page counters** (projects completed, collaboration partners, niche specialisation, certifications). No figures were visible to me. Ask for real numbers or leave them out.
4. **"Leading firm" claim** on About Us. It is not backed up on the page, so I left it out.
5. **Latest work.** Projects stop at 2023 and the footer says 2022. Ask for anything newer.
6. **Project details.** The project pages I could read have no written description, only "design stage" renders and "completion" photos. I could not load every project page (the site timed out on most), so check the rest. Types and locations below are my guesses from the names.
7. **Gallery inconsistencies.** The gallery lists Dato Ng, Charlton Martin and a "New project" that are not in the menu, and the menu lists INTI, which is not in the gallery. Some names point at differently named pages (for example, "Kota Kemuning Bungalow" opens Mr. Kwa KK Bungalow).
8. **Bugs to fix on the new site.** The "contact us" buttons on the Home and Services pages link to an external domain (fsguru.com). Facebook, Twitter and YouTube links on the Contact page are empty placeholders. There are typos ("Profesional", "It is not only conserves energy").
9. **People.** No founder or team is named. The email starts with "zul". Ask whether the principal should be named on the site.

**Structure decision.** Two pages only: Home and Projects. With no written project descriptions, each project should open as a **gallery with a Design stage / Completion toggle** (this matches how the client already organises its photos), so no separate project detail page is needed.

### Smart home questions (optional section above Contact, as a simple accordion)

*These answers are my plain-language drafts. The client should check them against the systems they actually install.*

- **What is a smart home system?** It connects things like lights, air-conditioning, curtains and security so you can control them from your phone, by voice, or on a schedule.
- **What makes one "future-proof"?** Planning the cabling and controls properly during the renovation, and choosing systems that can be updated or extended, so you are not opening walls again in a few years.
- **Why does it matter for interior design?** The best result comes when the switches, sensors and lighting are designed together with the room. Hidden wiring, tidy panels and well-placed controls keep the space looking clean.
- **What do I need to start a project?** Just a rough idea of what you want, your budget range and the address. We take it from there, with a free consultation and a site visit.
