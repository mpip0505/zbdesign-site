Smart home questions

What is a smart home system?

It connects things like lights, air-conditioning, curtains and security so you can control them from your phone, by voice, or on a schedule.

What makes one future-proof?

Planning the cabling and controls properly during the renovation, and choosing systems that can be updated or extended, so you are not opening walls again in a few years.

Why does it matter for interior design?

The best result comes when switches, sensors and lighting are designed together with the room. Hidden wiring, tidy panels and well-placed controls keep the space looking clean.

What do I need to start a project?

A rough idea of what you want, your budget range and the address. We take it from there, with a free consultation and a site visit.
